PixelFest 2027: Gaming Community Night

This form allows attendees to register for a fictional, free, one-day gaming event. Attendees can choose a session, indicate their gaming interests, and optionally express interest in tournament participation.

All information entered into the prototype will be fictional. No personal information will be collected, transmitted, or stored.

Data Minimization Summary

PixelFest 2027 collects only the information necessary to support event registration and participation. The form does not request home addresses, phone numbers, payment information, passwords, or birth dates.

Optional information is limited to gaming preferences, accessibility accommodations, and event-related questions. All test submissions will use fictional information.

Privacy Notice

This project is an academic front-end prototype. It does not transmit, store, or process actual registration data. No real personal information should be entered into the form.
